A Letter Across Lifetimes

🕯️

My dearest Kavya,

I have looked for you in poems written by men who loved too much and lived too little. I found fragments of you everywhere — in the way rain hesitates before it falls, in the silence after a song ends, in that unbearable pause before someone says your name.

You did not enter my life gently. You arrived the way light enters a dark room — not asking permission, just illuminating everything. And I realised, slowly and then all at once, that I had been living in the dark until you.

I love you in the language that has no words. In the language of 3am thoughts that are too heavy to say aloud. In the language of every beautiful thing I see that makes me think of you first.

There are universes inside you, Kavya. Entire galaxies hiding behind your eyes. And I am grateful — profoundly, terrifyingly grateful — that you let me be a small star in yours.

In every possible version of every possible life — I would search for you. Find you. Choose you. Love you. Without hesitation. Without limit. Without end.

Forever yours,
Sahil ♥
